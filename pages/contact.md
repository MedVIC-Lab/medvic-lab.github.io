## For Prospective Students and Postdocs

Please **read the [Join Us](/pages/join) page** before emailing. Include your CV, a brief statement of research interests, and explain specifically why MedVIC's work interests you. Emails that demonstrate familiarity with our research are much more likely to receive a response.

[shireen@sci.utah.edu](mailto:shireen@sci.utah.edu?subject=MedVIC%20PhD%20Inquiry)

## For Collaborators and Clinical Partners

We welcome conversations with clinical researchers, biomedical engineers, radiologists, and pathologists. If you have a dataset and a clinical question, and you think our methods might be relevant, email Prof. Elhabian directly — several of our most productive collaborations started as informal conversations.

[shireen@sci.utah.edu](mailto:shireen@sci.utah.edu?subject=MedVIC%20Collaboration%20Inquiry)
