# Jadie Adams, Ph.D.

**Research Scientist, Kitware Inc.**  
*PhD graduated 2024, University of Utah SCI Institute*

Jadie's dissertation research focused on probabilistic shape modeling from images and point clouds, producing the VIB-DeepSSM family and Point2SSM — among the lab's most-cited recent work. Point2SSM was accepted at ICLR 2024 as a Spotlight paper (top 5% of submissions).
