# Shireen Y. Elhabian, Ph.D.

**Associate Professor of Computer Science**  
Kahlert School of Computing | Scientific Computing and Imaging Institute  
University of Utah

Shireen Elhabian's research spans probabilistic machine learning, geometric deep learning, and generative modeling, with a focus on building AI systems for medical image analysis that are not just accurate but reliable, interpretable, and deployable in real clinical environments.

She received her Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Louisville in 2012 and joined Utah as a postdoctoral researcher in 2013, rising to her current tenured position in 2022.

She is the PI or co-PI on more than $52M in competitive research funding from NIH, NSF, ARPA-H, the VA, and the DoD, with over $11M under her direct leadership.
