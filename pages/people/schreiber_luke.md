# Luke Schreiber

Luke builds **research software for biomedical imaging** at the University of Utah's Scientific Computing and Imaging Institute. He leads full-stack development for **CranioRate**, an NIH-funded platform for AI-assisted craniofacial assessment, and contributes to cancer-cell analysis software used by Huntsman Cancer Institute. His work spans **product design**, **hospital implementation**, and **scientific visualization**.

Before joining the SCI Institute staff, Luke was a **Software Engineering Research Fellow**. He earned an **Honors B.S. in Data Science** from the University of Utah in 2024.

**Research:** Craniofacial imaging, clinical software, and visual analytics<br>
**In lab since:** 2024<br>
**Funding:** NIH-NIDCR
