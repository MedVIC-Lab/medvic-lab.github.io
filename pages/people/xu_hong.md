# Hong Xu, Ph.D.

**Assistant Professor, Rhodes College**  
*PhD graduated 2024, University of Utah SCI Institute*

Hong's dissertation research on deep learning and image analysis for shape modeling demonstrated how continuous representations can replace discrete landmark-based approaches at scale, including the Image2SSM and optimization-driven RBF shape modeling papers.
