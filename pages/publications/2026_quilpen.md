# QUILPEN Provides Independent and Label-Free Single-Cell Quantification of Pigmentation Dynamics and Organelle Content

**Rebecca G. Zitnay, Shukran Alizada, Tarek E. Moustafa, Devin Lange, Luke Schreiber, Alexander Lex, Robert L. Judson-Torres, Thomas A. Zangle, Rachel L. Belote**

*Pigment Cell & Melanoma Research, 2026*

## Abstract

QUILPEN combines quantitative phase, quadrant darkfield, and absorption imaging to measure pigment and organelle content independently in living cells. The label-free method supports multi-day imaging and lineage tracking, revealing heterogeneous and heritable patterns of melanin production during melanoma-cell repigmentation.
